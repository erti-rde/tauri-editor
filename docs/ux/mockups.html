Attention in Low-Resource Translation

1. Introduction

Sequence models built on recurrence dominated machine translation until attention-only architectures showed that parallel training could reach better quality at lower cost (Vaswani et al., 2017).

Pre-trained bidirectional encoders later extended this to a wide range of tasks (Devlin et al., 2019).

  • Two groups, counted. Your own notes come first, because they're what you meant to use.
  • Updates when the cursor leaves the paragraph or after a pause, never while typing.
  • The checkbox label that used to wrap becomes a header switch.
  • "Cited here" results sink, so the list stays about what you haven't used yet.

1 Introduction

Recurrent neural networks, long short-term memory and gated recurrent neural networks in particular, have been firmly established as state of the art approaches…

To the best of our knowledge, however, the Transformer is the first transduction model relying entirely on self-attention to compute representations of its input and output without using sequence-aligned RNNs or convolution.
